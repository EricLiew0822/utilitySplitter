Fair Utility Bill Splitting Explained

            

              Transparent mathematical formulas engineered for households with dedicated bedroom AC sub-meters. Eliminate group chat guesswork, guarantee exact kWh accountability, and keep shared areas equitable.
            

          

          

            
              tune
              Formula Sandbox
            
            
          

        

      


      
      

        

          

            

              STEP 01
              electric_meter
            

            
Sub-Meter AC Usage

            

              Every room with private air conditioning is recorded at the end of the billing cycle. Occupants pay strictly for the kWh their bedroom draws.
            

          

          

            (Current Meter − Previous Meter) = Room AC kWh
          

        


        

          

            

              STEP 02
              group_work
            

            
Common Area Share

            

              Total grid consumption minus all bedroom AC sub-meters equals common area usage (fridge, washing machine, lights, Wi-Fi), split equally across all N tenants.
            

          

          

            (Total Grid kWh − Σ Room AC kWh) ÷ N Tenants
          

        


        

          

            

              STEP 03
              currency_exchange
            

            
Rate & Carryover Surplus

            

              The effective rate rounds up to the nearest cent (e.g. RM 0.33/kWh) so the bill payer is never shortchanged. Any extra cents roll over to deduct next month's bill.
            

          

          

            ⌈(Electric Bill − Prev Carryover) ÷ Grid kWh⌉

Frequently Asked Roommate Scenarios

          

            
              What if someone goes on vacation for 2 weeks?
              expand_more
            
            

              Because bedroom air conditioning is sub-metered, their individual AC reading will register 0 kWh while they are away. Meanwhile, common area expenses (refrigerator running 24/7, Wi-Fi, base water fee) remain shared equally.
            

          

          

            
              Why is there a carryover balance, and who keeps that change?
              expand_more
            
            

              Rounding up to the nearest RM 0.01 prevents the bill representative from paying out-of-pocket for fractional cents. The small excess collected stays in the house pool and is automatically deducted from next month's electric bill.
            

          

          

            
              How are TNB progressive tariff blocks handled?
              expand_more
            
            

              By dividing the net TNB bill by total grid kWh, UtilitySplit derives the exact blended unit rate for that billing cycle so high-AC users pay proportionally for the kWh they consume.

UtilitySplit — House Utility Rules & Agreement

        
Fair Roommate Co-Living Charter & Sub-Meter Operating Procedure

      

      
    

    

      
Article 1: Sub-Meter Accountability. Each bedroom equipped with an electric sub-meter accepts full liability for kWh recorded between the start and end of the billing cycle.

      
Article 2: Shared Common Allocation. Common grid consumption is defined as Master TNB Bill kWh minus the sum of all bedroom sub-meters, divided equally among all active house residents.

      
Article 3: Effective Unit Rate. The unit rate equals Net Electric Bill (Gross Bill minus prior carryover surplus) divided by Total Grid kWh, rounded up to the nearest RM 0.01.

      
Article 4: Carryover Reserve. Any rounding surplus collected belongs to the house reserve and is automatically deducted from the following month's electric bill.
